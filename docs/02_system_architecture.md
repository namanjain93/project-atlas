# Atlas System Architecture

## 1. Overview

Atlas is designed as a modular quantitative research and investment intelligence platform.

The architecture separates data, research, prediction and portfolio construction so that each layer can be independently tested and improved.

## 3. Atlas Infrastructure

### Data Layer

Responsible for:

- Market prices
- Corporate actions
- Fundamentals
- Shareholding
- Macroeconomic data
- FII/DII flows
- Sentiment
- Other alternative datasets

### Research Layer

Responsible for:

- Literature
- Hypotheses
- Statistical testing
- Exploratory analysis
- Feature research
- Experimentation

### Feature Layer

Converts raw data into standardized research variables.

Examples:

- Earnings growth
- ROE
- Momentum
- Volatility
- Relative strength
- Valuation
- Sentiment scores

### Model Layer

Contains statistical and machine-learning models.

### Backtesting Layer

Tests strategies under realistic assumptions.

### Portfolio Layer

Converts stock-level predictions into portfolio decisions.

### Reporting Layer

Produces research outputs, charts, dashboards and reports.

---

## 4. Alpha

Alpha investigates:

> What information explains and predicts Indian equity returns?

Alpha is primarily a research and discovery layer.

---

## 5. Sigma

Sigma investigates:

> Can validated information be combined into useful forward-looking predictions?

Primary outputs:

- Probability of outperformance
- Expected return
- Downside risk
- Model confidence

---

## 6. Delta

Delta investigates:

> How should capital be allocated given the predictions and risks generated by Sigma?

Primary areas:

- Position sizing
- Diversification
- Sector exposure
- Risk limits
- Rebalancing
- Drawdown management

---

## 7. External Interfaces

The initial research environment will use:

- Google Colab for computation
- GitHub for version control
- Google Drive for large research files where appropriate

Future production infrastructure may use cloud databases, scheduled pipelines and monitoring systems.

---

## 8. Design Principle

No individual model should become the foundation of Atlas.

The infrastructure should allow models, features and hypotheses to be replaced without rebuilding the entire platform.
