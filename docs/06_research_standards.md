# Atlas Research Standards

## 1. Falsifiability

Every major hypothesis must be capable of being rejected by evidence.

---

## 2. No Look-Ahead Bias

Historical predictions may only use information available at the prediction timestamp.

---

## 3. Survivorship Bias

Historical universes must account for companies that subsequently entered or exited an index where the research question requires it.

---

## 4. Data Snooping

Repeatedly testing variations until finding a profitable result must not be presented as independent evidence.

---

## 5. Multiple Testing

Where many hypotheses are tested, the research must consider the probability of false discoveries.

---

## 6. Economic Significance

Statistical significance alone is insufficient.

A relationship must also be evaluated for economic relevance.

---

## 7. Robustness

Important conclusions should be tested across:

- Time periods
- Market regimes
- Sectors
- Alternative specifications
- Alternative benchmarks

where appropriate.

---

## 8. Benchmark Discipline

Complex strategies must be compared against simple alternatives.

---

## 9. Out-of-Sample Discipline

The final validation period should remain untouched until the methodology is sufficiently frozen.

---

## 10. Negative Results

Failed hypotheses are retained.

A failed experiment is valuable if it improves our understanding or prevents future research errors.

---

## 11. Human Judgment

AI-generated hypotheses, code and interpretations must be treated as proposals.

No AI-generated conclusion becomes an Atlas research conclusion without empirical validation.
